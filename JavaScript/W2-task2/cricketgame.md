Week 2 – Task 2
“Last Over”, a mini cricket chase game
You need 12 runs off 6 balls. Time your shot as the ball arrives, then screenshot your best finish and share it with friends.
1.	Show a pitch with a ball that moves toward the batter, and a Hit button (or Spacebar).
2.	Pressing at the right moment scores a 6, slightly early or late scores 4 or 1, and a miss is a dot ball or a wicket.
3.	The scoreboard shows runs needed, balls left, and wickets, and updates after every ball.
4.	A commentary line appears after each ball, such as “That’s gone into the stands!”
5.	The win or lose screen shows the result, with a Play Again button.
6.	Pick a difficulty (Easy / Medium / Hard) that changes the ball speed and the target.
7.	Save your best finish in localStorage.
